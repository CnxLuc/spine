Spine is ready

A calm reader for long articles. Here’s how to start.

  1. 1

    Pin Spine to your toolbar

    Select the puzzle piece at the top right of your browser, then the pin next to Spine.

    Pinned. Spine’s button is in your toolbar.

  2. 2

    Open Spine on a long article

    Select Spine’s button, or press ⌥⇧S. Press it again, or Esc, to go back to the page.

    Spine has no keyboard shortcut yet: another extension may use ⌥⇧S. Choose one.

    Try it on an essay
  3. 3

    Choose who reads

    The first time, Spine asks you, right in the reader:

    • ChatGPT, on your Plus or Pro plan. You sign in once, and there’s nothing to install.
    • Claude, on your Pro or Max plan. You paste one command into Terminal, or PowerShell on Windows, once.

    Spine sends the article’s text to the one you choose. It picks out the sentences that carry the piece in 15 to 60 seconds, and keeps its notes, so opening the article again is instant.

Change who reads, and everything else, in Spine’s settings. Read Spine’s privacy policy.
